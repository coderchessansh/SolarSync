01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Simple technology.
Useful where it matters.

SolarSync is a portable charging system created to address a practical problem: phone charging can become difficult when electricity is limited or unavailable.

Our kit uses a solar panel, charge controller, rechargeable battery and USB output. Sunlight becomes stored electrical energy that can be used later to charge a phone.
