01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Simple technology.
Useful where it matters.

SolarSync addresses a practical problem: phone charging can become difficult in rural areas, during power cuts, or anywhere reliable electricity is unavailable.

The kit combines a solar panel, TP4056 charge controller, rechargeable 18650 battery, MT3608 boost converter and USB output in a compact stand-mounted setup.
